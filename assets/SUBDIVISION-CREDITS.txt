US and Canadian subdivision data — retrieved 9 October 2026

Boundary geometry and representative capital coordinates: Natural Earth,
public-domain data. Inputs are pinned to natural-earth-vector commit
ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Transforms: selected only 50 US states and 13 Canadian provinces/territories;
rounded map coordinates to four decimal places; oriented rings for D3 spherical
geometry; retained geographic islands and actual world coordinates; derived
shared-boundary adjacency. Capital names were checked against US GPO and Canadian
government reference lists. Source URLs and source file hashes are in SUBDIVISION-SOURCES.json.

US flag SVGs: Flagpedia / FlagCDN state flag bundle, described by its provider
as public domain, based on Wikimedia Commons. The bundle includes the updated
Minnesota and Utah designs, visually checked during this build.
https://flagpedia.net/us-states/download
https://flagcdn.com/us-svg.zip

Canadian flag SVGs: Wikimedia Commons, fetched individually. Each file's returned
licensing metadata identifies it as public domain. Exact file pages, original
download URLs, author/credit text and licensing metadata are retained per flag
in SUBDIVISION-SOURCES.json. Preserve that file when redistributing the assets.

These notes describe source copyright metadata, not a grant to imply endorsement
or an assessment of separate restrictions on official insignia and emblems.
The map data is generalized educational cartography, not legal boundary evidence.
No population, land area or largest-city facts were inferred from older map data.

Australia additions: eight public-domain flags from Wikimedia Commons (per-file metadata in SUBDIVISION-SOURCES.json). Names and capitals checked against Australian Home Affairs citizenship material. Natural Earth boundaries use the same pinned source as US/Canada. Includes Lord Howe Island in NSW and Macquarie Island in Tasmania; external territories and Jervis Bay are not separate targets.

Files now live directly in assets/; no subdivision or flag subfolders are required.
